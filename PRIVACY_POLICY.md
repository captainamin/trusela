# Privacy Policy for Trusela

**Last Updated:** July 14, 2026

Trusela ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Trusela.

This Privacy Policy applies to our website, application, and its associated services (collectively, our "Service"). By accessing or using our Service, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information from you when you register on our Service, log into your account, or use our application.
- **Account Information:** When you create an account using Firebase Authentication, we collect your email address, name, and profile information.
- **Google Account Data:** To provide our core functionality, our application requests access to your Google Account. We specifically request access to:
  - **Google Drive:** To create a dedicated folder for your records and upload images (such as ID cards, device photos, and receipts) directly to your personal Drive.
  - **Google Sheets:** To create and manage a database spreadsheet where your business or market records will be safely stored.
  - **Basic Profile Information:** To associate your Google Account with your Trusela profile.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Any of the information we collect from you may be used in one of the following ways:
- **To provide and maintain our Service:** We use your Google Drive and Sheets access to store your own data on your own Google Account. We do not store your uploaded images or spreadsheet rows on our own central servers; they reside directly in your Google Drive.
- **To personalize your experience:** Your information helps us to better respond to your individual needs.
- **To improve our Service:** We continually strive to improve our application offerings based on the information and feedback we receive from you.
- **To process transactions:** If you subscribe to our premium plans (e.g., via Paystack), we use your information to verify and manage your subscription.

## 3. How We Protect Your Information

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information when you place an order or enter, submit, or access your personal information.
- **Authentication:** We use industry-standard Firebase Authentication to secure your login credentials.
- **OAuth Tokens:** Your Google OAuth refresh tokens are securely stored in our Firestore database so that the application can seamlessly sync your data to your Google Drive and Sheets without requiring you to log in repeatedly.
- **Data Ownership:** The records you create (sales records, market members, inventory) are written directly to a Google Sheet located in your own Google Drive. We do not share your private spreadsheet data with third parties.

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer to outside parties your personally identifiable information. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or servicing you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential. 

Our third-party service providers include:
- **Google (Firebase):** For authentication and database hosting.
- **Google (Drive & Sheets APIs):** For storing your application data.
- **Paystack:** For processing subscription payments.

## 5. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Trusela's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 6. Your Consent

By using our Service and authorizing the Google OAuth connection, you consent to our Privacy Policy and the permissions required to manage your Drive and Sheets data on your behalf.

## 7. Changes To Our Privacy Policy

If we decide to change our privacy policy, we will post those changes on this page, and/or update the Privacy Policy modification date at the top.

## 8. Contact Us

If there are any questions regarding this privacy policy, you may contact us using the support email provided in the application.
